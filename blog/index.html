The Hormuz Closure Is Rewiring Global Oil

19 August 2026

The Strait of Hormuz carried 130 to 140 vessels a day. Now it carries 10. The oil is rerouting — through Egypt's SUMED pipeline, to Venezuela, to discounted Iranian crude for China. The crisis is accelerating a structural shift in who sells oil to whom.
