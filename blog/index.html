What's on the radar

  • Iran ceasefire expires — no deal, no talks, no off-ramp

    The 60-day MOU signed June 18 expired Aug 17 with both sides at a complete impasse. Iran says it never entered the agreement. Trump at Aug 14 rally vowed to "soon" declare Hormuz US territory. Iran's deputy FM: "The Strait of Hormuz has been Iranian, is Iranian, and will remain Iranian." Bessent threatening "economic isolation like the world has never seen." Only 2 vessels transited Aug 14, no crude visible. The war has no diplomatic path forward.

  • Consumer sentiment at 51.0 — only 8% expect to get ahead

    Michigan sentiment plunged from 55.2 to 51.0 in early August. Expected business conditions sank 11% short-run, 17% long-run. Only 8% of consumers expect income to outpace inflation, down from 18% in December 2024. Year-ahead inflation expectations 4.3%. July retail sales fell 0.6%. The consumer is cracking.

  • Investors pile into ultra-short bonds — defensive shift accelerates

    Ultra-short bond ETFs saw $12.8B inflows in July. Money market ETFs $18.7B year-to-date. TLT averaging -6.7% annual return over five years. Advisors moving portfolios from 2% to 5% cash. "We've become more defensive as equity markets hit all-time highs." The smart money is hedging a record market.

  • China buying oil at lower prices — the swing buyer returns

    China's July crude imports jumped 22% from June's decade-low to 8.45M bpd as Beijing stockpiled at lower prices. But the economic backdrop deteriorated: retail sales +0.6% y/y (vs +1.5% expected), industrial output +4.5% (vs +4.8% expected). Q2 GDP grew just 4.3%, slowest since 2022. When China returns to international markets in force, oil bears will be in trouble.

Europe's Winter Is Two Crises at Once

17 August 2026

Gas inventories at a 17-year low. The Rhine at 24 centimeters. The drought is cutting hydropower and nuclear output while the gas supply is tighter than 2022. The EU is banning Russian LNG in January while buying record amounts now. Europe is walking into a dual energy shock with fewer buffers than it had four years ago.

The Emergency Buffer Is Gone

15 August 2026

The SPR fell below 300M barrels for the first time since the 1980s. Two wars in four years drained 352M barrels. Energy Dept officials told GAO they're "holding the SPR together with Band-Aids." The reserve that was built to cushion a supply crisis is nearly gone, at the moment global supply is most constrained.

The Diesel Crisis Is Structural, Not Cyclical

14 August 2026
