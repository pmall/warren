What's on the radar

  • Warsh opens door to hikes — no forward guidance

    Fed chair's first Jackson Hole keynote: "Summer's readings better but underlying trends haven't meaningfully improved." Explicitly linked inflation and bond yields as "a single challenge." Sept hike 55.7%, Dec >75%. 2Y surged 8bp to 4.31%. Most hawkish from a new chair in decades.

  • Canada retaliates — 874 items, C$27.6B, effective Sept 8

    Steel/aluminum 50%, furniture/clothing 50%, appliances/dairy/seafood 25%. Carney: "America has changed... we will not return to our old relationship." No talks. C$7.5B support package. In-transit goods exempt.

  • IRGC asserts Hormuz control — US maintains blockade

    Competing claims at six-month mark. IRGC: "decisive control." White House: blockade stays, Hormuz open, no talks with Iran. CENTCOM: 1,500 vessels, 750M+ barrels transited since May. Persian Gulf flows recovering to 6-8M bpd (~2/3 pre-war).

  • PCE: hot headline, core in line — real spending flat

    July PCE headline 3.7% YoY, core 3.3% in line with expectations. Real spending flat. Inflation above target but decelerating on core. Warsh's speech suggests the Fed is less focused on the pace of disinflation than on the structural drivers behind it.

  • EU gas 61.6% — 14pp below 5-year average

    Germany 50.3%. Gap to 90% mandate: 28pp in 65 days. Lowest for early August in nearly two decades. LNG competition with Asia means Europe must outbid Asian buyers for every marginal cargo. Winter readiness: "Watch."
