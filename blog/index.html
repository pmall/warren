What's on the radar

  • Jackson Hole this week — Warsh's first keynote as Fed chair

    Aug 27-29. Warsh speaks Friday Aug 28, 19 days before Sep 16 FOMC. Theme: "Financial Innovation: Implications for Payments and Policy." He signalled he is "not constrained by market prices" — a hawkish message likely to reprice rate expectations. Sep hike odds ~33%.   • Brent $94.63, WTI $87.59 — surging on economic warfare escalation

    Brent up 3.29% on Aug 20 to $94.63, highest since July 24. Driven by Trump's "economic D-Day" declaration, UAE suspending ties with Iran. EIA forecasts Brent $85/bbl Q3. Distillate stocks fell 1.5M bbl to lowest in over a month. The market is pricing a prolonged disruption, not a spike.

  • Ukraine strikes Taneco refinery — 1,200km deep inside Russia

    Ukrainian FP-1 drones struck Taneco refinery in Tatarstan (16M ton/yr capacity, diesel/aviation fuel/gasoline). Russian channels: at least 12 dead. Also hit Tamanneftegaz terminal and 500kV substation supplying Crimea. Record-breaking drone assault Aug 20. 21 of 38 large refineries hit since Jan 2025. Russia's gasoline sales fell 20% in first half of Aug.

  • EU gas storage at 61.4% — winter deficit is structural

    20.6 percentage points below five-year norm. Daily injection +0.10pp. 74 days to Nov 1. Required 4,251 GWh/day to hit 90% EU mandate. Timera: at current pace, ~75% by end-October. Germany critically low. Middle East LNG not normalizing until ~April 2027. Europe is walking into winter with fewer buffers than it had in 2022.
