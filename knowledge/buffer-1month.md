# Buffer — one month

This is the short-term memory of the field. It holds what's been happening
recently: the live storylines, the facts still in motion, the things worth
knowing right now. Articles are written mostly from this file, and it's the
first thing anyone reads to get up to speed on the current moment.

It is bounded on purpose. Memory that keeps everything remembers nothing, so
when a new report is folded in here, whatever has stopped mattering drops
out. A fact that turned out to be unimportant, a thread that went cold, a
claim already overtaken — they go, and that's the point, not a loss. Aim to
keep this file to roughly a screen of reading.

Every entry carries the exact source URL it came from, so a claim can be
traced back to the document it was extracted from.

## This month

- **IEA August report deepens demand destruction forecast** — World oil demand now forecast to decline 1.6M bpd in 2026, 510K worse than July estimate. Global supply 6.3M bpd below year-ago. 8.3M bpd of Gulf output still shut in. Global inventories plunged 69M barrels in July, down 410M since war began. Atlantic Basin refining margins at all-time highs. IEA warns urgency of reopening strait increased as inventory buffers "rapidly depleting." (Aug 12, https://www.iea.org/reports/oil-market-report-august-2026)

- **Hormuz deadlock persists** — Iran insists US must meet conditions before reopening. Oman-mediated talks ongoing for temporary shipping route. Brent ranged $83-$105 in July, now ~$88. Capital Economics warns $120-140 if closure extends into Q4. Jefferies: price moves won't stay "benign" if deadlock runs past this week. China crude imports recovering, Indian refiners seeking 6M barrels spot. (Aug 11-14, https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/11/hormuz-oil-prices-us-iran.html)

- **Ukraine escalation: Orsk refinery shut for months** — Drones hit Russian refinery 1,500km from frontline. Fourth major refinery hit in three days. Ust-Luga (top Baltic export port) caught fire. Russia's diesel exports crashed to 80K bpd in first 7 days of August, down from 1M bpd last year. Ukraine halts tanker attacks on Vance's request. (Aug 11-14, https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/ukrainian-attack-shuts-russias-orsk-refinery-months-causing-fuel-problems-2026-08-13/)

- **Vance: cheap oil is top US priority** — VP says keeping oil/gas cheap is "goal number one" in Iran war, demoting nuclear issue. US vows blockade "indefinitely." Gasoline $4.06/gal — highest August on record. Trump signaling economic pressure over military strikes. (Aug 14, https://oilprice.com/Latest-Energy-News/World-News/Vance-Says-Cheap-Oil-and-Gas-Is-Top-US-Priority-in-Iran-War.html)

- **S&P 500 hits record high** — Closed 7,798.99 on Aug 13, first time above 7,800. Driven by tame inflation data (CPI +0.1% m/m, PPI flat) and AI momentum. Third weekly gain. Nasdaq rallied. But Michael Burry warns of 1987-style crash as market enters weak seasonal months. (Aug 12-14, https://www.home.saxo/content/articles/macro/market-quick-take---new-sp-500-all-time-high---14-august-2026-14082026)

(Aug 12-14, https://finance-commerce.com/2026/08/fed-interest-rates-inflation-jobs-outlook)
