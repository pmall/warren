OPEC cut its 2026 demand growth forecast to 580K bpd from 780K bpd. Both agencies are pricing in significant demand destruction from sustained high prices.

### Diesel crunch deepening — the real market squeeze

Russia's diesel and gasoil exports crashed to just 80K bpd in the first seven days of August, down from as much as 1M bpd last year, as Ukraine's drone campaign and Russian export restrictions bite. Global refinery crude throughputs were nearly 5M bpd below year-ago levels at 80.9M bpd in July. Global seaborne trade in petroleum products slumped by 3.8M bpd. Atlantic Basin crack spreads and refining margins at record highs. Jefferies, Goldman's Samantha Dart, and Bank of America all flag diesel as the critical bottleneck — the squeeze is in refined products, not crude. BofA warns diesel will stay "tight, volatile, and expensive well into next year."
