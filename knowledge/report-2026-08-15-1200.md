# Report — 2026-08-15

## Hormuz / Iran War

- **Trump declares Hormuz will become US territory** — At a rally in New York on Aug 14, Trump vowed to "soon" declare the Strait of Hormuz a US territory after defeating Iran. Iran's Deputy FM Gharibabadi responded: "The Strait of Hormuz cannot be seized by a tweet or an aircraft carrier." Iran insists it will not reopen the strait until US meets conditions. No peace talks planned. (Aug 15, https://www.straitstimes.com/world/middle-east/iran-defiant-on-strait-as-trump-tells-americans-to-accept-high-gas-prices, https://www.aljazeera.com/news/liveblog/2026/8/15/iran-war-live-trump-vows-to-make-hormuz-us-territory-pretty-soon)

- **ADNOC vessels attacked in strait** — UAE state oil company ADNOC confirmed one of its vessels was attacked in the Strait of Hormuz on Aug 14, following two attacks on Aug 13. Saudi Arabia condemned the attacks, calling them "acts of piracy by Iran's Revolutionary Guard Corps." The UAE foreign ministry demanded "complete and unconditional reopening" of the strait. (Aug 15, https://www.cbsnews.com/live-updates/iran-war-donald-trump-strait-of-hormuz-uae-accuses-attacks-vance-oil-gas/)

- **Traffic collapses, ships pushed to Iran-controlled corridor** — Only 2 vessels passed through the strait on Aug 14 with no crude oil shipments visible (Kpler). 16 of 18 projectile strikes since July 6 hit the Omani corridor, pushing ships toward the northern, Tehran-controlled route. UKMTO counted 19 outbound vessels using the northern route vs. 8 on the southern route. Traffic ~90% below pre-war levels. (Aug 14-15, https://gcaptain.com/iranian-attacks-push-hormuz-shipping-toward-tehran-controlled-route/, https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/13/us-iran-war-trump-hormuz-irgc.html)

- **Bessent threatens "economic isolation like the world has never seen"** — Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent told Newsmax new measures against Iran coming next week. VP Vance reiterated cheap oil is "goal number one." Trump told rally it's worth paying "a tiny little bit more for gasoline." (Aug 14-15, https://www.cbsnews.com/live-updates/iran-war-donald-trump-strait-of-hormuz-uae-accuses-attacks-vance-oil-gas/)

- **Iraq exports rising via alternative route** — Iraq's average daily oil exports since the beginning of August are the highest since the war began, flowing via the southern corridor bypassing Hormuz. (Aug 15, https://www.cbsnews.com/live-updates/iran-war-donald-trump-strait-of-hormuz-uae-accuses-attacks-vance-oil-gas/)

## Oil Markets

- **Crude up on tanker attacks, no peace progress** — Brent settled at $88.52 (+$1.45, +1.67%), WTI at $82.40 (+$1.15, +1.42%). Weekly gains: Brent +6.0%, WTI +5.4%. "We're getting a rally going into the weekend after new attacks on tankers and lack of progress on a cease-fire agreement," said Lipow Oil Associates. (Aug 15, https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/markets/commodities/news/oil-inches-toward-90-on-tanker-attacks-lack-of-progress-in-iran-war-peace-deal/articleshow/133256494.cms, https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/14/oil-prices-today-brent-wti-hormuz.html)

- **Novorossiysk terminal shut after drone strike** — Crude oil exports from Russia's Sheskharis terminal at Novorossiysk suspended on Aug 14 after drone attack. Terminal capacity ~700K bpd. Exports had reached nearly 1M bpd in July. A tanker was forced to flee to open sea. (Aug 14-15, https://newsukraine.rbc.ua/news/key-russian-oil-terminal-shuts-down-after-1786726351.html, https://www.reuters.com/world/drones-hit-residential-buildings-russias-black-sea-port-novorossiysk-mayor-says-2026-08-12/)

- **Diesel crisis worsening** — US diesel futures up 7.4% to $4.19/gallon. Retail diesel $5.32/gal (up from $3.71/yr ago). Europe diesel up 40% since mid-June. Russian diesel exports crashed to 80K bpd in first 7 days of August (from 1M bpd last year). US distillate inventories at lowest for this time of year since 1996. "Diesel is the everything the industrial economy runs on." (Aug 10-15, https://oilprice.com/Energy/Energy-General/Global-Diesel-Crunch-Worsens-Ahead-of-Peak-Winter-Demand.html, https://oilprice.com/Energy/Energy-General/Refinery-Attacks-Deepen-Global-Diesel-Supply-Crunch.html, https://oilprice.com/Energy/Energy-General/Russias-Diesel-Exports-Crash-to-Multiyear-Low-amid-Tight-Global-Market.html)

- **Refineries running at max** — Exxon, Chevron at 95-97% utilization; Shell over 100%. "Refinery utilization above 90-95% simply means there is very little operational flexibility left." Maintenance season approaching, which will further reduce output. (Aug 12, https://oilprice.com/Energy/Energy-General/Refinery-Attacks-Deepen-Global-Diesel-Supply-Crunch.html)

- **US crude inventories rose 17.4M bbl last week** — Largest weekly increase in 3.5 years. Partially offsetting geopolitical premium. Storage holding up better than feared, per Julius Baer. (Aug 15, https://www.indexbox.io/blog/oil-prices-head-for-weekly-gain-amid-us-iran-tensions/)

## US Economy

- **Consumer sentiment plunges to 51.0** — Down from 55.2 in July, below forecast of 54.5. Biggest monthly drop in months. Expected business conditions sank 11% short-run, 17% long-run. Only 8% expect income to outpace inflation (down from 18% in Dec 2024). Year-ahead inflation expectations rose to 4.3% from 3.4% in Feb. Republicans steepest decline — 19% below pre-Iran conflict levels. (Aug 14, https://www.sca.isr.umich.edu/, https://qz.com/us-consumer-sentiment-august-2026-iran-inflation-081426)

- **Retail sales fell 0.6% in July** — Largest decline in 14 months. First drop in 9 months. Excluding gas/autos, down 0.2%. Nonstore retailers -2.2%, autos -1.8%. Clothing stores +1.9% (back-to-school). Goldman cut Q3 GDP forecast by 0.5pp to 2.2%. (Aug 14, https://www.census.gov/retail/sales.html, https://www.pbs.org/newshour/economy/u-s-retail-sales-unexpectedly-post-largest-drop-in-more-than-a-year)

- **Fed hold at 3.50-3.75%, divided FOMC** — 9-3 vote, three dissenters (Hammack, Kashkari, Logan) wanted hike. Markets now pricing ~69% chance of hold in September, down from 50% a month ago for hike. PCE inflation eased to 3.7% in June. (Jul 29-Aug 12, https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/monetary20260729a.htm, https://www.reuters.com/business/traders-stick-narrow-bets-september-fed-hold-after-inflation-data-2026-08-12/)
