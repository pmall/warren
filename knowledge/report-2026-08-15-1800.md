### U.S. Strategic Petroleum Reserve hits four-decade low, cavern integrity at risk

The SPR has fallen below 300 million barrels for the first time since it was filled in the early 1980s, with inventories set to sit around 243 million barrels once the 172 million barrel drawdown in response to the Iran war is finished. Experts warn the rapid drawdown risks damaging the salt caverns that store the oil. Siddharth Misra, a petroleum engineering professor at Texas A&M, said the "practical operational floor" is between 250 million and 300 million barrels — at current levels, "cavern integrity and overall operational capability are at an elevated risk." The system was originally designed for five full drawdowns but has executed dozens over 40 years. Energy Department officials told the GAO they are "holding the SPR infrastructure together with 'Band-Aids.'" Former Biden energy advisor Amos Hochstein said a hurricane at current levels could put the U.S. in a dangerous position. The DOE says at least 70 million barrels must remain, but Hochstein disputes that figure, saying going below 300 million risks permanent damage. (Aug 15, https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/15/strategic-petroleum-reserve-spr-oil-iran-war-caverns.html)

### Rystad cuts Russia crude forecast, warns industry "running out of room"

Rystad Energy revised its Russian crude production forecast to average 8.95 million bpd in 2026, declining to 8.6 million bpd in 2027 — a 90,000 bpd downgrade from prior outlook. Refinery throughput is forecast at 4 million bpd July-December, nearly 30% below the 2016-2023 seasonal average of 5.7 million bpd. Russia's spare production capacity sits at only ~620,000 bpd, with much tied to aging wells at growing risk of permanent abandonment. Onshore crude inventories are already at levels where sustained production cuts are difficult to avoid. The impact of drone attacks is no longer just refineries — it's constraining the upstream sector. Even if constraints ease, meaningful recovery in output appears unlikely. (Aug 15, https://oilprice.com/Energy/Crude-Oil/Russias-Oil-Industry-Is-Running-Out-of-Room-to-Absorb-More-Shocks.html)

### Russia's wartime economy shows cracks beneath the surface

Russia's GDP grew 1.3% y/y in Q2, its first growth since 2023, driven by military spending and higher oil prices. But analysts say headline data mask deeper strain. Oil and gas revenues in H1 2026 were 64% of their level two years ago. The deficit is on course to double from 2025 levels. Inflation was brought down to ~4% late last year but "will not last." Households are trading down — X5 Group, Russia's largest retailer, says cookie consumption rose 2.5x as citizens switch to cheaper foods. Eurasia Group's Alex Kolyandr: "If I were Putin, I would probably decide it is in my interest to escalate now and try to finish the war on my terms, than wait until the money ends." Analysts don't expect economic stress alone to end the war — but worsening conditions could incentivize escalation. (Aug 15, https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/15/ukraine-war-russia-economy-putin-oil.html)

### U.S. rig count rises as drillers respond to higher prices

The total U.S. active rig count rose to 593, up 54 from year-ago levels. Oil rigs +1 to 455 (43 above last year), gas rigs +4 to 128. Permian Basin rigs rose by 2 to 265, 10 above year-ago. U.S. crude production averaged 13.805 million bpd, up 521,000 bpd from a year ago. The rig build is a direct response to sustained higher crude prices, though the lag between drilling and production means this supply response won't be felt for months. (Aug 14, https://oilprice.com/Energy/Crude-Oil/US-Oil-Drillers-Add-Even-More-Rigs-As-Oil-Prices-Stay-Higher.html)

### Investors flee to ultra-short bonds as safety trade

Ultra-short bond ETFs saw $12.8 billion in inflows in July as investors take record equity gains off the table. Money market ETFs saw $18.7 billion in inflows from January-July. Bank deposits pay well under 1%. TLT has posted -6.7% average annual return over five years; IEF -1%. Financial advisors are moving model portfolios from 2% cash to 5%, building baskets of ultra-short ETFs. "We've become more defensive as equity markets continue to hit all-time highs." The shift reflects not just rate uncertainty but concern about the equity market's disconnect from economic reality. (Aug 15, https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/15/cash-money-market-funds-bonds.html)

### Somali piracy surges amid Hormuz blockade

Somali piracy is surging as the Hormuz blockade reroutes shipping and reduces naval patrols in the Gulf of Aden. The disruption to established shipping lanes is creating new security risks beyond the immediate conflict zone.
