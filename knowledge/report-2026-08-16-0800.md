### Reddit joins S&P 500, replacing AvalonBay Communities

Reddit (RDDT) will be added to the S&P 500 effective prior to market open on Tuesday, August 18, replacing AvalonBay Communities. The stock jumped ~11% after-hours on the announcement. Reddit is the second pure-play social media company in the index after Meta. Q2 2026 marked eight consecutive quarters of 60%+ y/y revenue growth, GAAP profitability, and 90%+ gross margins. 2025 total revenue surpassed $2B (+69% y/y), net income $530M. Market cap ~$29.5B. The inclusion is an off-cycle change prompted by a current constituent's acquisition. Reddit is now the #3 most visited website in the US. (Aug 13-14, https://www.nasdaq.com/press-release/reddit-will-be-added-sp-500-2026-08-14, https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/13/reddit-shares-jump-11percent-on-inclusion-in-sp-500.html)

### Trump vows to "soon" declare Hormuz US territory, ceasefire expires Monday

At a rally at a police academy on Long Island on Aug 14, Trump said: "After we finish defeating Iran, which is being very badly defeated — pretty soon I'll be declaring the Hormuz Strait a territory of the United States." He added: "It's true," with a chuckle. A White House official later clarified to WSJ that Trump was joking. Iran's Deputy FM Gharibabadi fired back: "The Strait of Hormuz has been Iranian, is Iranian, and will remain Iranian." The ceasefire is set to expire Monday (Aug 18) with no mention of extending it and no peace talks planned. The US-Iran MOU framework appears to have collapsed. (Aug 14-16, https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/16/trump-threatens-to-make-the-strait-of-hormuz-a-us-territory-can-he, https://english.alarabiya.net/News/middle-east/2026/08/15/trump-says-will-soon-declare-hormuz-strait-us-territory)

### Congressional Research Service publishes comprehensive Hormuz analysis

CRS released R45281 (updated Aug 7), a detailed analysis for Congress. Key findings: Iran established a "Persian Gulf Strait Authority" (PGSA) in May 2026 claiming no vessel can transit without a permit. Iran's speaker said "management of the Strait will never return to the way it was before the war." As of early August, conflict has abated amid Iran-Oman talks related to the Strait. The DFC's $20B maritime reinsurance facility has done $0 business. The report notes that while Iran's actions give it leverage, they also entail costs — renewed conflict with the US, reimposed blockade severely exacerbating Iran's economic crisis. (Aug 7, https://www.everycrsreport.com/reports/R45281.html)

### Diesel overtakes jet fuel in Europe as global shortage widens

Diesel prices have overtaken jet fuel in Europe — an unusual inversion reflecting the severity of the supply crunch. Europe has been pulling in jet cargoes from the US and other countries as prices surged. Global diesel supply tightened further when Russia banned exports amid Ukrainian attacks on its refineries. C.H. Robinson reports North America is better insulated by domestic production but diesel prices remain highly sensitive to global balances. Key story has shifted from outright shortage to managing geographic availability and inventories. (Aug 13-15, https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/diesel-prices-overtake-jet-fuel-europe-global-shortage-widens-2026-08-13, https://www.chrobinson.com/en-us/resources/insights-and-advisories/north-america-freight-insights/aug-2026-freight-market-update/diesel/)

### IEA August report: demand destruction deepening, supply falling further

IEA August report details: World oil demand forecast to decline 1.6 mb/d in 2026 (510 kb/d worse than July). Global supply rose to 101.5 mb/d in July but remained6.3 mb/d below year-ago. Gulf output still 8.3 mb/d shut in. Renewed hostilities in July-August reduced 3Q26 supply estimates by 1.7 mb/d vs last month. Global inventories plunged 69 mb in July, down 410 mb since war began. North Sea Dated ended July at $96.80/bbl, trading ~$92 at time of writing. Refinery throughputs 5 mb/d below year-ago. Atlantic Basin refining margins hit all-time highs. Product markets tightening with global seaborne petroleum product trade down 3.8 mb/d. (Aug 12, https://www.iea.org/reports/oil-market-report-august-2026)
