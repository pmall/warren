## SPR hits four-decade low — cavern integrity at risk

SPR fell below 300M barrels for first time since the 1980s. Set to sit at ~243M after 172M barrel drawdown finishes. Texas A&M prof Misra: "cavern integrity and overall operational capability are at an elevated risk." The SPR was designed for 5 full drawdowns — has executed dozens over 40 years. Fresh water pumped during drawdowns dissolves salt walls, creating "flatter, less stable roof and severely thins the critical salt pillars." Energy Dept told GAO they're "holding the SPR together with Band-Aids." Hochstein: hurricane at current levels could put U.S. in dangerous position. Rapidan Energy estimates "soft-ish floor" of ~170M barrels. (https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/15/strategic-petroleum-reserve-spr-oil-iran-war-caverns.html)
