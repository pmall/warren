## Hormuz: US military claims 660M barrels aided since May, but reality remains contested

US Central Command told CNBC that US forces have helped 1,300 commercial vessels transport more than 660 million barrels of crude through Hormuz since early May — implying ~7M bpd over the past three weeks. Energy Secretary Wright cited ~9M bpd seven-day average as of Aug 11. Windward estimates crude exports averaged ~5M bpd in July (up from 4M in June, 1.6M in May).

But the picture is far more complicated. Al Jazeera's analysis of Kpler data shows that of 112 crude/LPG/LNG tankers through Hormuz Aug 1-19, only 2 formally used the Omani route while 21 openly used the Iranian route — and 89 (80%) went dark or unclassified. Al Jazeera's conclusion: "fear of offending Tehran appears to outweigh Trump's threats." Chinese shipping giants carrying half of pre-war Middle East imports have kept tankers out of Hormuz and Bab el-Mandeb since late July. Daily transits remain volatile: 7 ships on Aug 20, down from 14 the day before. No VLCCs or LNG carriers among the seven.
