## Nvidia +8.7% to ~$228 — market digests earnings, embraces growth story

After initially falling 1.6% after-hours on Aug 26, NVDA reversed sharply and closed up 8.7% at ~$228. Market decided the growth outlook justifies margin compression. CFO Kress guided FY28 revenue growth ~70% (vs Street's 44%), calling memory scarcity "a symptom of the same demand surge that is driving our own growth." CEO Huang said demand "is much greater than 70%." Jensen Huang passed Zuckerberg and Ellison in billionaire ranks. Separately, Nvidia agreed to acquire Hugging Face for $12.9B. (Aug 27, https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/26/nvidia-nvda-earnings-report-q2-2027-live-updates.html, https://www.forbes.com/sites/tylerroush/2026/08/27/jensen-huang-passes-zuckerberg-and-ellison-in-billionaire-ranks-as-nvidia-pops-9/)
