# Report — August 28, 2026 (12:00 ET)

## Warsh Jackson Hole keynote: "In Our Time" — opens door to hikes, rejects forward guidance

Fed Chair Kevin Warsh delivered his first Jackson Hole keynote Friday morning (10am ET). Title: "In Our Time." Key takeaways: (1) No forward guidance — "You can call it an outline, a trail map, just don't call it forward guidance." (2) No reaction function — explicitly declined to commit to a rule. (3) Inflation concerns dominant — "while this summer's readings were better than expected, they do not tell me that underlying trends have meaningfully improved." Said "we have work to do" if inflation doesn't improve. (4) Called for a "quieter Fed, more purposeful in its communications." (5) Said Fed "should not indulge a regime in which market participants are looking primarily to the Fed for their next trade." (6) Economy "appears to have strengthened." (7) Did not mention Bessent's Treasury buyback program. Market reaction: 2-year yield surged 8bp to 4.31% (highest since late July). Sept hike probability jumped to 55.7% — up ~20pp from a day ago. Stocks climbed modestly after speech. (Aug 28, https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/28/kevin-warsh-jackson-hole-federal-reserve-inflation.html, https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/warsh20260828a.htm, https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-28/key-takeaways-from-fed-chairman-warsh-s-jackson-hole-speech)
