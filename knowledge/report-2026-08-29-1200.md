### Canada publishes detailed retaliation list — 874 items, C$27.6B (Aug 25-27)

Ottawa released full product-level schedule on Aug 25: 874 tariff items covering C$27.6 billion in U.S. goods, at 15%, 25%, or 50% — each rate matching the U.S. rate on the corresponding product. Steel and aluminum jump to 50%, furniture and clothing at 50%. Appliances, dairy, seafood at 25%. Effective 12:01 a.m. September 8. Key structural difference: in-transit goods exempt (unlike U.S. Section 338). Ottawa also unveiled C$7.5 billion support package. PM Carney: "America has changed... we will not return to our old relationship." No talks scheduled. (Aug 25-27, https://www.canada.ca/en/department-finance/news/2026/08/list-of-products-from-the-united-states-subject-to-counter-tariffs-effective-september-8-2026.html, https://www.freightfigures.com/articles/canada-retaliation-tariffs-september-8-2026)
