## ADP: Private payrolls 38K, below estimates

ADP reported private payrolls rose by 38,000 in August, below the 47,000 estimate and down from July's upwardly revised 46,000. Slowest month since January. Manufacturing lost 17,000 jobs; professional/business services off 16,000. Growth concentrated in education/health (+45K), leisure/hospitality (+16K), construction (+12K). Pay gains steady: base pay +3% YoY for stayers, gross pay +4.4%. BLS nonfarm payrolls due Friday — consensus 53,000 after July's -23,000. Unemployment rate forecast 4.1%. (Sep 2, https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/02/private-payrolls-rose-by-38000-in-august-fewer-than-expected-adp-reports.html)
