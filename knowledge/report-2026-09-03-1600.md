## Iran-Hormuz: Escalation deepens — Iran strikes Kuwait & UAE, claims new mines

Iran launched its 31st phase of Operation Saeqeh on Sep 3, striking US bases in Kuwait (Ahmed Al Jaber Air Base) and the UAE (Al Minhad Air Base) with missiles and drones. Kuwaiti air defenses engaged; the foreign ministry called the strikes "a direct threat to its security." Iran claimed it damaged satellite communications, equipment warehouses, and fighter jet hangars. Separately, IRGC spokesman Ebrahim Zolfaghari claimed Iran laid additional naval mines along "illicit routes south of the Strait of Hormuz" overnight, mocking Trump's suggestion to rename it "Trump Strait." CENTCOM previously said all mines were cleared and dismissed mine reports as "disinformation." Iran's First VP Aref warned of "dark months" for the US economy and said future responses would be "asymmetric" and "multi-layered." The Strait remains at 4% of typical transit volume (3 vessels Aug 23 vs ~85/day normal). Polymarket: 3% chance of normal by Sep 30, 28% by Dec 31.
