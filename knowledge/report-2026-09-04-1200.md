## Goldman Sachs: "dark ship" traffic creates 5M bpd data gap at Hormuz

Goldman strategist Yulia Zhestkova Grigsby warns Gulf oil exports have rebounded to 15-16M bpd, but visible shipping data captures only ~10M bpd — a daily gap of ~5M bpd from tankers disabling AIS transponders ("dark ships") to evade Iranian tracking. After incorporating dark ship data, Goldman estimates net shock to Persian Gulf flows at ~7.9M bpd — still major but far less than visible data suggests. This implies ~39M barrel upward revision to global crude inventory data, meaning actual supply-demand balance may be looser than headline figures. Meanwhile, Saudi crude exports plunged to ~3M bpd in August — 9-year low — as both Red Sea (Houthi blockade on Yanbu) and Persian Gulf routes face simultaneous pressure. Brent above $95. (Sep 4, https://finance.biggo.com/news/b80fa747-f6d6-4cf5-840d-06480ed53035)
