# Report — 2026-09-04 20:00 UTC

## NFP: +162K blows the doors off — hike odds spike, Waller's hold case crushed

August nonfarm payrolls came in at +162K, nearly triple the +56K consensus (range +42K to +65K). July revised UP from -23K to +21K, June revised UP from +20K to +31K — net revisions +55K higher. Unemployment 4.1% (unchanged). Labor force participation rebounded to 61.6% from 61.4%. Average hourly earnings +0.3% MoM, 3.1% YoY. Leisure and hospitality surged +62K (restaurants +59K). Manufacturing +16K. Construction +22K. Local government education +42K reversed July's decline. Capital Economics: "Even the most committed dove would struggle to find anything to justify keeping rates unchanged." Citigroup pushed back next rate cut forecast to June 2027. (Sep 4, https://www.reuters.com/business/us-nonfarm-payrolls-surge-august-unemployment-rate-steady-41-2026-09-04/, https://www.bls.gov/news.release/empsit.nr0.htm)

## Hike odds jump to 65% — Waller's "if" just got harder

CME FedWatch jumped to ~65% Sep hike from ~55% before the report. 10Y yield rose to 4.78% (+3bp on the day), reversing two days of decline. Dollar strengthened. Waller's statement on Sep 3 — "if there is continued progress toward our 2% goal, then I am willing to support holding" — now faces a much higher bar. The NFP beat validates ING's framing: the default is hike, and burden is on soft data to prevent it. Waller himself said Aug CPI (Sep 11) would be decisive, but today's data makes a hold nearly impossible unless CPI comes in dramatically soft. (Sep 4, https://tradingeconomics.com/united-states/government-bond-yield, https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/04/gold-holds-ground-with-us-payrolls-data-on-radar.html)

## Gold hammered — tests $4,300 support, -2% on NFP

Gold fell more than 2% on the NFP beat, testing the critical $4,300 support level. 50-day EMA at ~$4,384, 200-day EMA at ~$4,336. The combination of a strong labor market, rising hike odds, and higher yields crushed the gold thesis that was building on Waller's dovish comments. Yields rising and dollar strengthening is the worst combination for gold. The $4,300 level is the line in the sand — below it, the correction thesis toward $4,215 (WGC estimate) gains force. Sep 4, https://www.usatoday.com/story/money/personalfinance/2026/09/04/gold-price-on-september-04-2026/91607539007/)

## Oil steady at $91/$95 — Hormuz still constrained

WTI settled at $91.30 (+0.32%), Brent at $95.52 (-0.12%). Six commodity vessels transited Hormuz Wednesday, down from 11 Tuesday, 10-day avg ~13.
