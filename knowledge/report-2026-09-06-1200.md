## OPEC+ meets Sunday — likely unchanged, Q4 left wide open

OPEC+ core seven (Saudi, Russia, Iraq, Kuwait, Algeria, Kazakhstan, Oman) met Sunday at 1100 GMT. Reuters sources said policy likely unchanged for October. Key context: actual production has consistently lagged planned quota increases — export disruptions in Gulf, Russia, Kazakhstan have broken the link between targets and supply. The gap between approved quotas and actual market supply has made each successive hike look bigger on paper than it's felt in practice. A separate 2M bpd layer of 2022 cuts stays in place through year-end.
