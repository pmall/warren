# Report — 2026-09-08 1800

## Iran-Hormuz

- **Houthis launch broad offensive on Saudi Arabia — 73 wounded, energy facilities hit** — Yemen's Iran-aligned Houthis attacked four Saudi southern cities (Abha, Khamis Mushait, Jazan, Najran) with ballistic missiles and drones on Sep 8. Saudi energy ministry confirmed fires at several energy facilities and operations temporarily halted. 73 people wounded including women and children. Jazan refinery (400K bpd) — already shut from previous attack — was targeted again. NASA satellite imagery showed large black smoke plumes from Abha bulk plant and Jazan sites. Saudi-led coalition vowed "firm response" calling it a "dangerous escalation." Houthi military spokesperson described it as a "broad offensive" against the OPEC leader. Brent crude rose $1 to $98/bbl after the statement; WTI up $2+ to $93.65. Goldman Sachs modestly raised oil-price forecasts, risks "significantly tilted to the upside." (Sep 8, https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/saudi-led-coalition-yemen-says-73-injured-houthi-attacks-kingdom-2026-09-08, https://www.nytimes.com/2026/09/08/world/middleeast/saudi-arabia-yemen-energy-attack.html, https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/08/oil-prices-today-brent-wti-hormuz-iran-war.html)

- **Qatar warns of "industrial catastrophe" if Hormuz crisis persists** — Qatar's Foreign Ministry spokesperson Majed Al-Ansari told CNN that opening the Strait of Hormuz is a priority, warning of an "industrial catastrophe" if the crisis continues.
