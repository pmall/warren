## PPI August 2026

- **PPI final demand +0.4% MoM, +5.4% YoY — in line, but energy drives the story** — BLS released August PPI at 8:30 AM ET Sep 10. Final demand +0.4% MoM (consensus +0.4%), July revised UP from unchanged to +0.1%. YoY jumped to 5.4% from 4.8%. Energy prices +4.2% MoM (renewed Iran hostilities pushing oil higher after two months of decline). Producer goods +1.1%. Core PPI (less food, energy, trade) +0.3% MoM, +4.7% YoY.
