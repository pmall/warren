## Hormuz / Iran — Diplomacy collapses, dual chokepoint squeeze intensifies

- **Oman meeting postponed** — The Salalah conference between Iran and Gulf Arab states, scheduled for Monday Sep 14, was postponed "in the interests of consensus" (Omani FM Badr Albusaidi). Only Iraq had confirmed attendance; Bahrain refused outright to meet Iranian officials. Saudi Arabia and Qatar occupied an uneasy middle ground. Iran's Fars news agency confirmed Tehran and Muscat jointly agreed to the delay after several regional governments requested it. Oman had proposed a 50-50 joint management arrangement for Hormuz with voluntary transit fees modelled on the Strait of Malacca — the proposal that was to be discussed. Satellite imagery shows sections damaged by blazes. Riyadh has not disclosed full extent of damage or given a repair timeline. Traders and Saudi oil buyers told Reuters that Yanbu stocks can sustain exports for only 5-7 days (~Sep 18-19 exhaustion). If not reopened, up to 4% of global oil supply could be at risk. Sources give conflicting repair estimates: days to weeks; one source said 5-6 weeks.
