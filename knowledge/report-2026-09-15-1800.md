## Diplomacy — Oman GCC-Iran talks postponed, Trump claims Ukraine-Russia energy truce

- **Oman postpones GCC-Iran Hormuz talks "in interests of consensus"** — The planned Sep 15 meeting in Salalah between GCC foreign ministers and Iran's FM Araghchi on managing Hormuz shipping was postponed Sep 14.
