## Key developments

### FOMC decision at 2pm ET today — hike virtually certain

The Fed is expected to raise rates 25bp to 3.75%-4.00% — the first hike since July 2023 and the first under Chair Kevin Warsh. CME FedWatch: 92-95% probability. The decision lands alongside the SEP (dot plot, GDP, inflation, unemployment projections) and a Warsh press conference at 2:30pm ET. Morgan Stanley switched from no hikes to two hikes (Sep + Dec). Goldman, JPMorgan, HSBC, Deutsche Bank, Morgan Stanley all forecast 25bp. The hike defies public pressure from Trump, Vance, and Bessent to cut. Trump threatened to "suspend trade" with countries running surpluses if the Fed didn't cut. Warsh's Jackson Hole speech opened the door to hikes; NFP +162K and hot core CPI (+0.3% MoM) sealed it. Key question: does the dot plot show one-and-done or a series? (Sep 16, https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/16/fed-meeting-today-live-updates.html, https://www.reuters.com/business/warshs-words-may-matter-more-than-anticipated-fed-rate-hike-2026-09-16/, https://www.nytimes.com/2026/09/16/business/economy/federal-reserve-interest-rates-warsh.html)

### Saudi pivots to Hormuz spot sales after pipeline shutdown

Saudi Aramco sold ~20M barrels to Asian refiners this week via ship-to-ship transfers off Oman's Sohar port (outside Hormuz). Loadings at Yanbu suspended. European customers told some September cargoes cancelled. East-West pipeline shut since Sep 11 after drone attacks from Iraq's Maysan province. Rystad: pipeline likely offline 3-5 weeks. Yanbu stocks last 5-7 days (~Sep 18-19 exhaustion). The pipeline was carrying 4-5M bpd — Saudi's primary Hormuz bypass. Without it, Saudi effectively has no high-capacity land bridge to Red Sea. Bloomberg: Saudi now selling Arab Light, Arab Medium, Arab Heavy for STS loading off Sohar. (Sep 16, https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-16/saudis-pivot-to-send-more-oil-through-hormuz-after-pipeline-shut, https://www.channelnewsasia.com/world/saudi-arabia-east-west-pipeline-oil-crude-exports-houthis-red-sea-6382731, https://oilprice.com/Latest-Energy-News/World-News/Saudi-Arabia-Pivots-to-Spot-Oil-Sales-After-Key-Pipeline-Goes-Offline.html)

### Oil retreats — Brent $107, WTI $104 — on Saudi spot sales + API inventory build

Brent fell $1.69 (-1.55%) to $107.06, WTI fell $2.60 (-2.46%) to $104.13. Pullback driven by: (1) Saudi offering crude via Oman easing supply fears, (2) API data showing US crude +7.1M bbl vs -1.6M expected, gasoline and distillate also up. But Yanbu suspension + pipeline outage + Hormuz filtering + Houthi Red Sea blockade all remain. Russia set to extend diesel export ban until end of October (Vedomosti). Libya force majeure risk after Hamada-Zawiya pipeline valve closure. (Sep 16, https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/oil-falls-us-crude-inventories-rise-despite-saudi-supply-concerns-2026-09-16/)

### Diesel $6.31/gal — new all-time high, California near $9

AAA: national diesel average $6.31/gal, new all-time high for second consecutive day. DOE/EIA weekly: $6.285/gal. Up ~69 cents from Aug 31. Up ~72% YoY. California $8.21/gal; Bay Area stations near $9/gal. ULSD futures $5.12/gal. Diesel up ~60% since Iran war began. Russia extending diesel export ban through October. Trucking industry parking trucks over operating feasibility. Heating season and agricultural demand approaching. (Sep 16, https://usa.news-pravda.com/world/2026/09/16/885895.html, https://abc7news.com/post/california-diesel-prices-hit-record-highs-bay-area-stations-nearing-9-gallon/19836676/, https://www.truckingdive.com/news/diesel-prices-surge-past-628-per-gallon/830416/)

### 10Y yield closes above 5% — highest since 2007

10-year Treasury yield touched 5.041% intraday (highest since July 2007), closed at 5.008%. First close above 5% since 2023. 30Y ~5.34%. Higher for longer now official Fed policy — the bond market is pricing in the hike plus more ahead. Mortgage rates, auto loans, credit cards all face upward pressure. (Sep 16, https://hdfcsky.com/news/nasdaq-falls-205-points-dow-sheds-328-as-treasury-yield-hits-5-041percent-september-16-2026)

### Gold rebounds to $4,328 — resilience despite rate hike

Gold +0.8% to $4,328 after scaling one-month low on Monday (~$4,261). Broke through $4,340 resistance with volume. Commerzbank notes gold's resilience is surprising given 92% hike odds — fiscal concerns and geopolitical risk providing floor. Key question is Warsh's forward guidance. Resistance $4,366, support $4,307 and $4,261. (Sep 16, https://www.reuters.com/world/india/gold-muted-investors-brace-fed-rate-decision-2026-09-16/, https://www.investing.com/analysis/gold-just-broke-4340-hours-before-the-fed-speaks-200687782)

### Ukraine-Russia energy ceasefire: no deal, strikes continue

Trump claimed Sep 14 that both sides agreed to halt energy strikes. Neither side confirmed. Zelenskyy: Ukraine ready if Russia reciprocates and US guarantees. Kremlin: "good idea" but still being discussed. Overnight into Sep 15: both sides launched new energy strikes — Russia hit petrol stations in Kyiv, Ukraine struck Syzran refinery (Rosneft). Reuters analysis: even if ceasefire holds, it won't fix the diesel crunch — refinery damage is too extensive. Russia extending gasoline export ban through year-end, diesel ban non-producers. (Sep 16, https://www.nbcnews.com/world/ukraine/russia-ukraine-new-energy-attacks-trumps-ceasefire-claim-rcna597873, https://www.euronews.com/my-europe/2026/09/16/ukraine-ready-for-energy-ceasefire-but-russia-has-yet-to-agree-zelenskyy-says)
