### Fed HIKES 25bp to 3.75-4.00% — unanimous 12-0, signals one more this year

The FOMC voted unanimously (12-0) to raise the federal funds rate by 25bp to 3.75-4.00% on Sep 16 — its first hike since July 2023. Chair Kevin Warsh said inflation is "too high for too long" and the committee "must be confident that underlying inflation is moving to our objective clearly and at sufficient speed." The SEP dot plot shows 16 of 18 participants expect at least one more hike this year, with 4 seeing two more — median year-end projection of 4.125% (one more 25bp). Four officials see 50bp additional, two see no more hikes. Warsh again declined to submit his own dot. Officials nudged up 2026 PCE inflation forecasts to 3.7% headline, 3.4% core (both +0.1pp from June). Inflation target not expected until 2029. No cuts penciled for 2027; one cut each in 2028 and 2029. Jim Bianco noted long-term rates rising after the completion of a rate cut cycle is the first such occurrence in over 50 years — the market had been demanding hikes for two years. Mortgage rates jumped to 7.19%. Post-decision: S&P 500 fell 0.45% to 7,551.81, Dow -1.21% to 51,462, Nasdaq essentially flat. 10-year yield moved back above 5% to 5.016%. ### Ukraine energy ceasefire: both sides launch strikes, no deal

Both sides launched new energy attacks overnight despite Trump's Sep 14 claim of an energy truce. Russia fired 200 drones at Ukraine, hitting energy and port facilities; one killed in Kyiv, seven injured. Ukraine struck Syzran oil refinery (Rosneft) and drone facilities in Taganrog and Oryol. Zelenskyy told CBS he is "ready for energy ceasefire" but it must cover "any type of weapon on energy" — both Russian oil/gas and Ukrainian electricity/gas infrastructure. Ukraine awaiting Russia's list of facilities. Kremlin spokesman Peskov called it "a very good idea" but linked it to lifting sanctions and ensuring tanker safety — effectively a counter-demand. Both sides described the other as not having agreed. Situation is a rhetorical ceasefire with continued kinetic operations.
